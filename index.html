Fair Valuation

How we assign value

Vehicle Details

The year, make, and model help determine your vehicle's base value. Different trims and features are also taken into account.

Mileage & History

We consider the number of kilometers on your vehicle, CarFax, available maintenance and service records.

Vehicle Condition

We look at the overall interior and exterior condition of your vehicle. Rust, damage, and signs of wear are taken into account.

Market Demand

We compare your vehicle to similar vehicles on the market. Current prices and demand are considered as part of the appraisal.
